Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

VoiceInk ("the App") is developed by MohAspiringDev ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use VoiceInk.

1. Information We Collect

Audio Data

VoiceInk records audio through your device's microphone when you initiate a recording session. Audio is captured solely for the purpose of transcription.

Transcription Data

Your transcription history is stored locally on your device. We do not upload or store your transcriptions on any server controlled by us.

Usage Data (Advertising)

We use Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google processes this data.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Audio processing: Recorded audio is sent to third-party cloud transcription services (Google Gemini API and/or OpenAI API) to convert speech to text. Audio is transmitted over an encrypted connection and is not retained by us after transcription is complete.
  • Local storage: Transcription results are stored locally on your device so you can access your history.
  • Advertising: Usage data is collected by Google AdMob to serve ads within the App.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect data:

  • Google Gemini API - for cloud-based speech transcription. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • OpenAI API - for cloud-based speech transcription. Subject to OpenAI's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob - for serving advertisements. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage and Security

All transcription data is stored locally on your device. We do not maintain external servers or databases that store your personal transcription content. Audio data is transmitted securely (via HTTPS) to third-party APIs for processing and is not stored by us.

5. Permissions

  • Microphone: Required to record audio for transcription.
  • Internet: Required to send audio to cloud transcription services and to load advertisements.

6. Children's Privacy

VoiceInk is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Your Choices

  • You can delete your local transcription history at any time from within the App.
  • You can revoke microphone permissions through your device settings.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the App or on this page with an updated revision date.
